2. How we use it

2.1 We use your data to: create and secure your account; run the services you use (rooms, events, travel buddies, job referrals, experts, sales and ads); check what we say we check; take payments and give refunds; keep members safe and enforce our Terms; send service messages; send marketing only if you agreed; and fix and improve the Service.

2.2 Legal bases. We rely on your consent (for example marketing), on what is needed to provide the Service you signed up for, on our legitimate interest in keeping the Service safe and free of fraud, and on legal obligations such as tax records. You can withdraw consent at any time; this does not affect what we did before.

2.3 Moderation and automatic safety checks. New photos may be checked automatically for nudity and sexual content by Google Cloud Vision, which processes the photo in memory only, does not store it and does not use it to train its models; we keep only the result. Text that asks for money, bank or payment details, gift cards or money transfers outside the app's payment steps may be blocked; we keep only a note that it was blocked. Our team reviews content that is reported or flagged. We do not read messages for advertising or any other purpose.

5. Security

We protect your data with encryption in transit and at rest, access limited to people who need it, row-level security in our database so each member can reach only what they are allowed to, private storage for photos and ID images, and passwords stored only as salted hashes. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach puts you at real risk of significant harm, we will tell you and the relevant regulator as the law requires.

10. NeuAmigo services (Settle In, Events & Friends, Jobs, Membership, For Business)

10.1 What we collect for these services, only when you use them:

  • Events and leagues: the events you book, number of seats, ticket code, check-in time, team name and points. Hosts see your name and ticket, never your phone number or email.
  • Find a Room: what you post (title, area, rent, dates, photos) and the contact details and address you choose to add. These are shown to NeuAmigo Plus members in the same country.
  • Travel Buddy: from and to city, flight day and, if you add it, flight number; once matched, the buddy's progress steps. The matched family and buddy see each other's first name and these trip details.
  • Jobs: for employers, the jobs you post (title, pay, hours, area, what you need); for job seekers, the Work Profile you fill in (skills, languages, the days you can work, area, and, only if you choose, whether you may work in that country). Employers see your skills and area; your name and contact go to an employer only when you apply or say yes. We do not check any of this.
  • Businesses (hosts, experts, agents, event services, advertisers): business name, city, category, about text, services and prices, licence number if you add it, plan dates, and the requests members send you.
  • Connect me / Request booking: when you tap it, we share your name and email with that one business, and keep a record that you did.
  • Moving-Out Sale: item, price, pick-up area, buyer and seller, and the status of the sale.
  • Weekend Club: your membership dates, the seats you book (adults and kids), and your season points. Organizers see your name, seats and ticket code.
  • Seat Credits and request wallet (businesses): credits and wallet balance, what was used and for which booking or request.
  • Documents you choose to share: if you upload a document for another member (for example a licence or ID), we store it and show it as "shared by the member, not checked by NeuAmigo" to the people you choose. We do not check it. You can delete it at any time.
  • Your country: the country you live in sets the prices and currency you see.
  • Price requests ("Get 5 prices"): what you need, the area and the date. We send it to at most five businesses in that category; they see your first name and the request, and your contact only after you reply or accept.
  • Payments: what you bought, amount, currency, date and the reference from PayPal or, later, our payment partner. We never see or store your full card or bank numbers.
  • Ads: we count how many times each ad was shown and tapped, in total. We do not tell advertisers who you are.

10.2 Why: to provide the service you asked for, to take payments and pay providers, to prevent fraud and keep members safe, and to meet tax and accounting law (legal obligation and contract).

10.3 Who we share it with: the other side of the service as described above; PayPal or, later, our payment partner, to process payments; and authorities where the law requires.

10.4 How long: room posts end after 30 days and are deleted 90 days later; trips are deleted 12 months after the flight day; price requests and Connect requests 12 months after they were sent; jobs and applications 12 months after the job closes; Work Profiles when you delete them or your account; payment records are kept for 8 years because tax law requires it. When you delete your account, all of this goes too, except payment records, and except events a business already ran, which stay under the name "Deleted business" so that buyers keep their ticket history.

10.5 Your rights in section 6 apply to all of this data.
