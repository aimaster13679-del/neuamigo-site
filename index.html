Questions

Which cities is NeuAmigo in?

You pick your city when you open the app. Homes, jobs and events come from people and businesses in that city, so some cities have more than others while we grow.

Do I have to pay to use it?

You can look around without paying. Contact details, chat and some extras come with a plan. Asking businesses for prices costs you nothing: the businesses pay.

Is NeuAmigo a matrimony app?

No. NeuAmigo helps with everything around a new life abroad. For marriage, our separate app is NeuMarry.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Me, then Settings and help, then Delete my account. Or see the delete account page.
